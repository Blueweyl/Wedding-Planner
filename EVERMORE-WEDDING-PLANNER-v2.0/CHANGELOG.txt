EVERMORE WEDDING PLANNER — CHANGELOG


VERSION 2.0 — Smart Concierge                                  October 2026

New
  * Smart Concierge tab, plus a "next steps" card on Home. Reads your whole
    plan and ranks what matters: overdue and upcoming vendor payments, RSVP
    follow-ups, missing meal choices, the caterer headcount, over-budget
    categories, vendor quotes that exceed their budget line, overdue and
    current checklist phases, essential vendors not yet booked, vows, and
    backups. Works fully offline; nothing leaves your computer.
  * Your next three actions, with one-click shortcuts. Any shortcut that
    changes your plan shows the change first and waits for confirmation.
  * Budget savings ideas matched to your biggest categories, and what each
    extra guest costs in catering.
  * Ask the Concierge: plain questions ("How much do we still owe?") answered
    from your own plan, offline.
  * Copy-ready friendly RSVP reminder.
  * Optional AI assistant using your own API key (Anthropic Claude tested;
    OpenAI, Google Gemini, OpenRouter and local models via a connection
    test). Planning advice, vow drafts, vendor emails and open questions.
    Off by default, asks for consent before sending, shows exactly what is
    sent, never sends guest names, contacts or your diary, and never changes
    your plan without confirmation. See AI-SETUP-GUIDE.html.
  * Automatic safety copies: daily, and before every restore, reset or
    accepted Concierge/AI change. Restore any of them from Settings.
  * Undo after restoring a backup or a safety copy.
  * Add your own Timeline tasks, and remove ones that don't apply.
  * Include your vows in the printed keepsake.
  * "Last backup saved" shown in Settings; dated backup and CSV file names.

Fixed
  * DATA SAFETY: if saved data could not be read, v1.0 promised to leave it
    alone but overwrote it with the next change. It is now copied aside
    first and can be downloaded.
  * A single damaged value (an impossible date, a non-number amount) no
    longer makes the whole plan unreadable; just that value is reset and you
    are told how many were.
  * After the wedding date passed, Settings refused to save a name change.
  * Amounts were rounded to whole units, so a 50-cent balance showed as
    "paid in full". Money is now exact to the cent everywhere (JPY stays in
    whole yen), and totals no longer drift from decimal rounding.
  * Amount fields no longer flag figures that aren't multiples of 50.
  * Impossible dates such as 31 February were accepted and silently became
    a different day.
  * "Today" (the default diary date) used UTC, so evenings in the Americas
    and mornings in Asia filed entries under the wrong day.
  * A currency label in an edited backup file could inject markup.
  * CSV export now opens accented names correctly in Excel.
  * Warnings no longer redraw on every keystroke.

Improved
  * Typefaces are now built into the file: identical look offline, and no
    requests to any font server.
  * Strict Content Security Policy: the page can only connect to the AI
    providers listed in Settings, and only when you use the assistant.
  * Text contrast raised to meet WCAG AA; a "skip to planner" link; dialogs
    trap focus and close with Escape; labels on every control.
  * On phones the tab bar scrolls sideways instead of stacking three rows.
  * Restoring a backup tells you whose plan it is, when it was saved, and how
    many guests and vendors it holds before you confirm.
  * A note appears if the plan is changed in another tab at the same time.
  * Printing any tab leaves out the navigation and warning bars.

Compatibility
  * Opens your existing v1.0 plan automatically — no import needed.
  * v1.0 backup files restore into 2.0, and 2.0 backup files restore into
    1.0.
