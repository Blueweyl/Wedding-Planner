Evermore
Optional AI Assistant · Setup Guide · Version 2.0

You do not need this to use Evermore. The planner and its Smart Concierge work fully offline, for free, forever. This guide is for couples who would also like an AI model to write vow drafts, vendor emails and personalised advice, using their own account with an AI provider.

1. What you get

On the Concierge tab, below your next three actions, the assistant offers four things:

  • Planning advice — a short, personal read of your plan: what to do next and why, risks it can see, and realistic ways to save. Any to-dos it suggests can be added to your Timeline after you tick the ones you want.
  • Write our vows — a draft in the tone you chose on the Vows tab, built from your own notes and (if you allow it) your story. You choose which vow section it goes into, and you see it before it goes in.
  • Vendor message — a ready-to-send email to any vendor: an inquiry, a request for an itemised quote, a polite negotiation, a confirmation or a thank-you. Copy it, or open it in your email app.
  • Ask anything — open questions, answered with your plan in mind.
The AI never changes your plan on its own. It only writes text. Anything that would go into your planner — vow words, new tasks — is shown to you first and only added when you press the confirm button. A safety copy is kept beforehand and there is an Undo straight after.

2. What it costs

Nothing from us. There is no Evermore subscription and no Evermore account. You pay the AI provider directly, per use, on their own pricing. For the kind of short requests Evermore sends, that is typically a few cents per answer; the faster models (Claude Sonnet and Haiku) cost less than the default.

Most providers let you add a small prepaid credit and set a monthly spending limit. Please set one — it means a lost or shared key can never surprise you.

3. Get an API key

We recommend Anthropic Claude: it is the provider we have tested working directly from the planner file in Chrome and Edge.

1
Create an account

Go to console.anthropic.com and sign up. This is a developer account, separate from a Claude.ai chat subscription.

2
Add a little credit and a spending limit

Under Billing, add a small amount of credit. Under Limits, set a monthly cap you are comfortable with.

3
Create a key

Open API keys → Create key, name it "Evermore", and copy it. It starts with sk-ant-. Keep it private, like a password.

4. Connect it to Evermore

  1. Open the planner and go to Settings → Optional AI assistant.
  2. Tick Turn on the AI assistant.
  3. Leave the provider as Anthropic Claude, and pick a model: Opus 5.5 writes best, Sonnet 5.5 is faster and cheaper, Haiku 5.5 is quickest and cheapest.
  4. Paste your key into API key.
  5. Decide whether to tick Remember my key on this computer (see section 7).
  6. Press Test connection. This sends one tiny message with none of your plan in it. When it says Connected, the assistant appears on the Concierge tab.

The first time you send a request, Evermore shows what will be shared and asks you to agree. You can open Preview this request there, or press See exactly what is sent any time, to read the exact text.

5. Using the assistant

Go to Concierge, scroll to AI assistant, choose what you want, and press Send. Answers usually take a few seconds to half a minute. You can press Cancel while it is thinking.

  • Copy puts the answer on your clipboard.
  • Use in my vows… lets you pick a vow section and whether to add to it or replace it.
  • Review suggested tasks… lets you tick the to-dos you want and choose the Timeline phase.
  • Open in my email app appears for vendor messages when that vendor's contact is an email address. Nothing is sent until you press send in your own email app.

AI can be confidently wrong. Treat what it writes as a first draft, and check prices, dates and anything contractual yourself.

6. What is shared — and what never is

You control most of it in Settings → What the assistant may see:

  • Budget figures (on by default)
  • Vendor names, booking status and amounts (on by default)
  • Your first names (off by default — vows and emails use placeholders instead)
  • Your story, only when writing vows (on by default, and you can untick it per request)

Always included, because advice is useless without them: the wedding date, guest counts (never names), the open items on your checklist, and the Concierge's own priority list.

Never sent, whatever you tick: guest names, meal choices, plus-one names, vendor phone numbers and email addresses, and anything in your diary.

Changing any of these settings, or switching provider, means Evermore asks for your agreement again before the next request. Withdraw data consent in Settings does the same at any time.

What the provider does with requests is governed by their own terms; check their privacy policy if this matters to you. Anthropic states that, by default, it does not train its models on API requests.

7. Keeping your key safe

  • Unticked (the default): your key is held only until you close the planner's tab. Paste it again next time. Safest on a shared computer.
  • Ticked: your key is stored in this browser on this computer, so it is there next time. Anyone who can use this computer and browser could use it too — which is why a spending limit matters.
  • Your key is never written into a backup file, never into a safety copy, and never sent anywhere except to the provider you chose.
  • Forget key in Settings removes it from this computer. If you think a key has been seen by someone else, delete it in your provider's dashboard and make a new one.
  • Evermore, and its seller, never see your key or your plan. There is no server for them to go to.

8. Other providers and local models

Settings also lists OpenAI, Google Gemini, OpenRouter and a local model on this computer (Ollama or LM Studio). For these, type the model name exactly as your provider shows it.

Not every provider accepts requests made straight from a file in your browser, and providers change this from time to time. We have tested Anthropic Claude this way. For the others, Test connection tells you on your own computer whether it works; if it does not, the assistant simply stays off and nothing else is affected.

Local models keep everything on your computer. Run Ollama or LM Studio, start its server, and enter its address (for Ollama, http://localhost:11434/v1/chat/completions). Ollama needs permission to answer a web page: quit it, then start it with the setting OLLAMA_ORIGINS=*. For security, Evermore only accepts addresses on this computer (localhost or 127.0.0.1).

9. Troubleshooting

"Your API key was not accepted"

The key was mistyped, cut short, deleted, or belongs to a different provider. Copy it again in full and paste it into Settings, then test again.

"The provider doesn't recognise the model"

Check the model name in Settings. For providers other than Anthropic, copy it exactly from their model list.

"You've hit a rate or spending limit"

You have used up your credit or reached your monthly cap, or sent many requests very quickly. Check billing in your provider's dashboard, or wait a minute.

"Couldn't reach …"

Check your internet connection first. If you are online, the provider is probably refusing requests from a browser file — switch to Anthropic Claude, or a local model.

"You're offline"

The assistant needs the internet. The Smart Concierge, and everything else, keeps working without it.

It is taking a long time

Larger models can take up to a minute on a long request. Press Cancel and try a faster model if you prefer. Evermore gives up by itself after two and a half minutes.

10. Turning it off

Untick Turn on the AI assistant in Settings. Press Forget key first if you want the key removed from this computer too. Your plan is untouched either way.
