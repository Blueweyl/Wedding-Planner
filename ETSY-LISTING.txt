3. DESCRIPTION
------------------------------------------------------------

Plan your whole wedding in one file. Budget, guest list, vendors and timeline
in a single planner that opens in your browser, works with no internet, and
never asks for a subscription or a login.


WHAT YOU GET

One HTML file that opens in any browser on your computer. Double-click it and
you are planning. Nothing to install, no account, no monthly fee, and your
plan never leaves your own computer.

- Budget tracker. Set your total, add what you are spending on, and see what
  is left as you go. Fifteen currencies included.
- Guest list. Names, RSVP, meal choice, plus-ones and plus-one names, with a
  running headcount. Export to CSV any time for your caterer or venue.
- Vendor payments. Total cost, deposit, paid so far and the balance for every
  vendor, with due dates and clear overdue warnings.
- Timeline. Your wedding date drives everything, so the planner shows you what
  is due now, what is coming and what has slipped.
- Countdown, notes and vows, plus a printable keepsake page.


HOW IT WORKS

1. Download the zip and unzip it.
2. Open Evermore-Wedding-Planner.html -- it opens in your browser.
3. Put in your names and your date. Start planning.

Your plan saves automatically as you type, right there in your browser. Close
it, come back tomorrow, it is all still there. A one-click backup file lets you
move your plan to another computer or keep a copy somewhere safe.


WHY NOT A SPREADSHEET

No formulas to break. No columns to widen. No Google account. No one else can
see your budget. It is built for the job, so the maths is already done -- your
balance, your headcount and your overdue payments update themselves.


WHAT IS INCLUDED IN YOUR DOWNLOAD

- Evermore-Wedding-Planner.html -- the planner
- START-HERE.html -- a plain-English guide, no jargon
- LICENSE.txt -- personal use terms

Instant download. The files are yours the moment payment clears.


PLEASE READ BEFORE BUYING

- This is a browser-based planner, not a Canva, Notion, Google Sheets or Excel
  template, and not a printable PDF planner.
- Designed for a laptop or desktop computer. Chrome, Edge, Firefox, Safari and
  Brave all work.
- Your plan is stored by your own browser on your own computer. It is private,
  and it is also yours to look after -- save a backup file, which takes one
  click and is explained in the guide.
- Clearing your browser data can erase the plan, exactly like clearing it
  erases a saved website login. The guide covers this on page one.
- Colours, fonts and layout are fixed. This is a working planner, not an
  editable design template.
- Digital files, so no refunds once downloaded. Message me if anything does not
  work and I will sort it out.


QUESTIONS

Do I need internet? Only to download it. After that it works with no
connection at all.

Do I need to install anything? No. It opens in the browser you already have.

Can my partner use it too? Yes -- send them the file, or your backup file, and
they have their own copy.

Is my information private? Completely. Nothing is uploaded anywhere. There is
no account and no server.

Can I print it? Yes. Print or save as PDF from your browser.


Thank you for supporting a small shop. If this planner takes one thing off
your mind, tell me -- and if something is not right, message me first and give

7. PRICING
------------------------------------------------------------

Launch at 9.99 USD for your first 10 sales, then hold at 14.99.

Under 5 reads as throwaway. Over 20 invites comparison with planners that come
with 60 printable pages. The first 10 sales are worth more than the margin:
reviews are what move you up the results page.

Run a 20 percent sale for the first two weeks. Etsy shows the strikethrough,
and the badge earns the click on its own.


------------------------------------------------------------
8. WHAT ACTUALLY MOVES YOU UP
------------------------------------------------------------

- Reviews. More than anything else. Follow up politely after each sale.
- Click rate. Photo 1 does this job alone.
- Staying. New listings get a short visibility boost. Do not delete and
  relist -- edit in place and let it settle.
- Finishing the sale. A listing people open and leave sinks. That is what the
  "please read before buying" section protects you from: the wrong buyer
  never buys, so they never leave a two-star review saying it was not Canva.

Check Shop Manager > Marketing > Search analytics after two weeks. It shows
the exact phrases people typed to find you. Put the winners in your title and
tags, and drop whatever brought nobody.
