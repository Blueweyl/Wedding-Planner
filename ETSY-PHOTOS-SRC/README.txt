PHOTO SOURCE FOLDER

Drop one photograph in here — jpg, png or webp — and re-render the listing
images. The first file in alphabetical order is the one used.

It appears in two places:
  photo 1  a full-width band across the top, behind the title, under a
           graduated cream scrim so the headline stays readable
  photo 10 a small tilted framed print in the lower right

Leave this folder empty and the images use the drawn gold rings, linen weave
and eucalyptus instead. Both looks are finished work; the photo simply reads
warmer.

Whatever goes in here must be a photograph the shop may use commercially:
its own, or a free-licence one from Unsplash or Pexels. A photograph of a
real couple needs their permission — a stranger's wedding photo in a paid
listing is a problem however beautiful it is.
