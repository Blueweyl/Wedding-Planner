ABOUT THE BACKGROUND

The linen weave, the gold rings and the eucalyptus are drawn in code, not
photographed. No stock licence to buy, no photographer to credit, no
AI-image question on the listing, and the tone matches the planner's own
palette exactly.

If you would rather have a real photograph behind the title — rings on linen,
florals, a couple — send me the image in chat and I will place it properly:
darkened where the text sits, blurred, cropped to 2000 x 2000. Use your own
photo, or a free-licence one from Unsplash or Pexels (both allow commercial
use, and Etsy listing images count as commercial).

A photo of a real couple must be one you have permission to use
commercially. A stranger's wedding photo in your listing is a problem even
when it is beautiful.

JPEG rather than PNG, because the woven texture pushes a PNG to about 3.7 MB
an image and a JPEG holds the same picture in about 400 KB.


WHAT WAS TAKEN FROM THE COMPETITOR LISTINGS, AND WHAT WAS NOT

Taken, because it is craft rather than property: the display serif title, the
warm neutral ground, layered laptop-and-phone mockups, callout labels on
leader lines, badge pills.

Not taken, because none of it is true here: "Bestseller" (that badge is Etsy's
to award, not a listing image's to claim), customer and review counts, Excel
and Google Sheets, 21 tabs, a YouTube tutorial, printable page packs, a
seating plan, a photo gallery.

Every feature named in these ten images is one the planner has. The over-plan
warning in photo 4 and the overdue payment in photo 7 are the real thing,
captured on screen rather than drawn on.


VIDEO (optional, but it earns clicks)

Five to fifteen seconds of your own screen: open the Guests tab, type a name,
add it, and let the headcount tick up. Movement in a grid of stills is what
makes a thumb stop.
