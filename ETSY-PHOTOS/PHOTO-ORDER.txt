WHAT THESE IMAGES CLAIM, AND WHY

Every line is something the planner does. There are deliberately no
testimonials, because you have no buyers yet and invented reviews are both
against Etsy's rules and the fastest way to lose a shop. Add a real review
panel once you have real reviews — it will outperform any made-up one.

There is also no mention of Excel or Google Sheets, printable page packs, a
video tutorial, a seating plan or a photo gallery. Those belong to a different
product. If you want any of them, they are a build, not a caption.


VIDEO (optional, but it earns clicks)

Five to fifteen seconds of your own screen: open the Guests tab, type a name,
add it, and let the headcount tick up. Movement in a grid of stills is what
makes a thumb stop.
