WHAT WAS TAKEN FROM THE COMPETITOR LISTINGS, AND WHAT WAS NOT

Taken, because it is craft rather than property: the display serif title, the
warm neutral ground, layered laptop-and-phone mockups, callout labels on
leader lines, and badge pills.

Not taken, because none of it is true here: "Bestseller" (that badge is Etsy's
to award, not a listing image's to claim), customer and review counts, Excel
and Google Sheets, 21 tabs, a YouTube tutorial, printable page packs, a
seating plan, a photo gallery.

Every feature named in these ten images is one the planner has. The over-plan
warning in photo 4 and the overdue payment in photo 7 are the real thing,
captured on screen rather than drawn on.


VIDEO (optional, but it earns clicks)

Five to fifteen seconds of your own screen: open the Guests tab, type a name,
add it, and let the headcount tick up. Movement in a grid of stills is what
makes a thumb stop.


ONE THING TO CHECK FIRST

Photos 1, 2 and 9 show the planner on a phone. Those are real renders at phone
size, and the phone route needs your hosted link working:
evermore-planner-k4m9x2.netlify.app — open it on your own phone before you
publish, so the photos are a promise you have personally checked.
